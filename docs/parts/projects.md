### Full Shift: a T-shirt store for students who work

[Live site](https://konradsikorski5.github.io/is117-brand-studio-ks2334/) · [Code (GitHub)](https://github.com/KonradSikorski5/is117-brand-studio-ks2334) · [Research](https://github.com/KonradSikorski5/is117-brand-studio-ks2334/blob/main/Research.md) · individual project

- **Who it's for:** students who go from class straight to a shift. I picked them after comparing Buck Mason, ASKET, and Uniqlo, because none of those stores spoke to that audience.
- **Two options I compared:** a clean modernist design and a bold postmodernist one, with the same words in both. I chose the bold yellow one. The modernist one felt "too simple," and the yellow one "looked like a site I would buy from."
- **What I did:** chose the audience, the brand name, the promise, the logo idea, and two honest persuasion choices. **The code was written with AI help (Claude).** I ran the checks and browser tests.
- **What feedback changed:** a classmate (Safwaan) asked for fabric numbers. I said no, because the shirt is fictional and I won't make up claims, and he agreed. He also asked about keyboard users, so I added a test that fails if the focus outline goes missing. My professor asked me to remove "comfortable" claims I couldn't prove, and I did.
- **Still to do:** build the checkout and save orders in a SQLite database (due Nov 23).
